A surface for a single idea — mono kicker, Caslon title, Newsreader support — on cream with a hairline border.

```jsx
<Card kicker="Feature" title="The art of the slow page">
  An essay on pacing, margins, and leaving space empty.
</Card>
```

Pass `accent` to recolor the kicker (e.g. `var(--color-secondary)`). All three slots are optional; omit `children` for a compact card.
